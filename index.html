Scanning Tips

  • Screen Brightness: Set sender screen brightness to 100%.
  • Fill Viewfinder: Position camera so the QR code fills 60%–80% of the box.
  • Steady Camera: Hold steady and avoid reflections or harsh glares.
  • Frame Density: If sender is at 2953B and camera is blurry, lower sender density to 1465B or 1000B.
